Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Starter, Standard and Premium?
Starter is free and perfect for small businesses just getting started with basic inventory tracking & Account Tracking with limited Orders. Standard is designed for growing businesses that need unlimited items and orders.Multi-user access. Premium includes everything in Standard plus external integrations, order management, sales team tracking with agent-wise features, Multi-location support, and mobile app access.
Can I upgrade from Starter to Standard or Premium?
Absolutely! You can upgrade anytime directly from your dashboard. Your data will be preserved, and we'll help you migrate seamlessly to your new plan without any downtime.
Is there a contract or long-term commitment?
No long-term contracts! Monthly plans can be canceled anytime with no penalties. Yearly plans offer 30% savings and are also non-binding after the first year. You're always in control.
What is Agent wise Sale and Credit tracking?
Agent wise tracking allows you to monitor each sales agent's performance individually - how much they've sold, collected, and their outstanding credit. This feature is only available in the Premium plan and helps you manage commission payouts and team performance effectively.
Can I use the software on multiple devices?
Yes! All plans support multiple device access on desktop and web browsers plus real-time sync across all devices.
